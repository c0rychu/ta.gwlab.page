# Project rules

## Chapters are mine

Never create, edit, or delete `latex/chapters/ch*.tex` unless I ask for it
explicitly in the request at hand. These files are the prose I am writing; a
helpful-looking insertion costs me more to find and undo than it saves.

This holds even when a change seems like the obvious next step -- e.g. after
adding a figure under `latex/fig/`, hand me the `\tikzfig{...}` snippet to paste
instead of putting it in the chapter yourself. Everything else in the repo
(`latex/fig/`, `latex/gwbook.cls`, `site/`, `Makefile`, ...) is fair game under
the usual rules.
